## Full description *(max. 4000 chars)*

Staccato – The modern app for music schools, private teachers and music associations.

No more paper lists, spreadsheets or WhatsApp groups. Staccato brings everything you need for your music teaching onto one clear platform – for teachers, students, parents and board members.

──────────────────────────────
🎵 FOR TEACHERS & SCHOOLS
──────────────────────────────
• Course management – individual, group, choir and ensemble classes with weekly scheduling, room planning and flexible billing
• Schedule – week view or list, colour-coded, for every role
• Attendance – record, edit and evaluate with a single tap
• Member management – profiles, documents, payment data and guardians in one place
• Invoicing – GoBD-compliant invoices (§14 UStG) with automatic numbering and PDF export

──────────────────────────────
📡 LIVE TEACHING
──────────────────────────────
Start a live session with a QR code. Students open the page on their phone and see sheet music, chords and lyrics in real time – and react with emojis. Attendance is recorded automatically. Also works for guests without an account.

──────────────────────────────
🎼 REPERTOIRE & TOOLS
──────────────────────────────
• Sheet music, audio, ChordPro chords and lyrics – all per piece
• Live transposition and capo calculator built right in
• PDF export with school logo
• Built-in tuner for everyday teaching

──────────────────────────────
🎻 FOR ASSOCIATIONS & BOARDS
──────────────────────────────
• Board module with goals, tasks and meeting minutes
• Inventory management with automatic numbering
• Events: concerts, recitals, exams – with RSVP and their own repertoire

──────────────────────────────
💬 COMMUNICATION
──────────────────────────────
Direct, school-wide and course messages – with push notifications and read receipts.

──────────────────────────────
🏫 FOR EVERY SIZE
──────────────────────────────
Whether you're a solo teacher, a medium-sized music school or an association with a board – Staccato grows with you. Multiple locations or schools can be managed centrally.

──────────────────────────────
🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
──────────────────────────────
All data is stored exclusively on servers in Germany. GDPR-compliant with data export and account deletion on request.

──────────────────────────────
✦ USER ROLES
──────────────────────────────
Staccato supports 6 roles with individually tailored views:
Admin · Teacher · Student · Parent · Board Member · Super Admin
